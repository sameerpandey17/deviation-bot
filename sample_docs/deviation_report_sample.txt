API Manufacturing Unit | Confidential

Date of Occurrence: 14-07-2024
Batch Number: API-2407-RX
Product: Ibuprofen Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient
Source: Manufacturing
Title: Reactor Temperature Excursion During Synthesis Step 3

DETAILED DESCRIPTION:
During the synthesis of Ibuprofen batch API-2407-RX on 14-Jul-2024, the 
jacketed reactor temperature rose to 92°C against an approved operating range 
of 70°C–80°C. The excursion lasted approximately 25 minutes before the 
operator manually adjusted the cooling water flow. Approximately 48 kg of 
in-process material was affected. No visible change in colour or odour was 
noted at the time of the deviation. The batch has been placed on hold 
pending QA investigation.

Root Cause (Initial Assessment):
Cooling water supply valve V-204 appears to have malfunctioned due to 
possible actuator failure. Maintenance to assess.

Severity: Major
Initial Impact: Product Quality
