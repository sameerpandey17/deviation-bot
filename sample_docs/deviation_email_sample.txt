Subject: Deviation – Temperature Excursion in Cold Storage Room C2

Hi QA Team,

I am writing to report a deviation observed in our cold storage facility.

On 18-Sep-2024 at approximately 11:15 AM, the temperature in Cold Storage 
Room C2 (designated for storing raw material RM-ACETYL-02, Acetylsalicylic 
Acid) was found to be 12°C instead of the required 2°C–8°C range.

Batch / Lot affected: RM-ASA-2409 (approx. 200 kg)

The alarm system did not trigger as expected; it was discovered during 
routine temperature logging by the warehouse team. The refrigeration unit 
compressor was found to be in a fault state. Engineering has been informed.

The material has been quarantined and tagged. We estimate the material 
was outside the temperature range for a maximum of 3 hours.

Please advise on the next steps. We request an urgent risk assessment.

Severity assessment (our initial view): Critical — Patient Safety risk 
given that this is a pharmaceutical raw material intended for tablet 
production for cardiac patients.
